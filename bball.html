    How a Trade Becomes Final

    The statutory path of a single two-for-one trade through the Government of Fantasia, compared with the same decision made by the Central Vibes Intelligence Office.

    1. Offer is submitted at the Playtime Bazaar and logged by the Souk of Unsolicited Offers.
    2. The Beef Color Guard checks whether the trade crosses an active rivalry line.
    3. The Trade Review Clock Authority starts the review period.
    4. The Missing Cookie Division opens a preliminary collusion inquiry, by default.
    5. The Office of Prismatic Intent issues seven opinions on what the trade means.
    6. The Senate of the Emeriti asks both managers why.
    7. Means and Ways confirms both parties have paid dues. They have not.
    8. The Public Relations Factory drafts a press release. The Cheeky Whelk Division rejects it.
    9. Foreign Affairs notifies the green-text nations. The message fails to deliver.
    10. The Cultural Ambassador's office considers a tribunician veto and schedules a meeting about it.
    11. The Commissioning Affairs Bureau forwards the file to the Commissioner, signed "Acting Dean."
    12. The Commissioner approves.
    13. The Assembly complains. An appeal is filed with the Supreme Tribunal of the Substratum.
    14. The Magistrate General upholds the trade.
    15. The Department of the Gone but Not Forgotten reacts with a thumbs-up. The Secret Society is believed to have known the outcome in advance.
    Elapsed time: 3 weeks · Both players now injured
